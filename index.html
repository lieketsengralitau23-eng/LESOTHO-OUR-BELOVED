Welcome

Lesotho is the only country in the world that is completely higher than 1,000 metres. It is surrounded by South Africa and is famous for its mountains, its blankets and its friendly people.

Look at the destinations, find places to stay, choose activities, see the gallery or book a trip.

what you should know!!

  • Capital: Maseru
  • Money: Loti
  • Languages: Sesotho and English
  • Highest peak: 4000 m

Travel tips

Fly to Maseru or drive in from South Africa. Carry your passport.

Warm clothes, a rain jacket and strong shoes. It snows from June to August.

The Loti and the South African Rand are both accepted.
