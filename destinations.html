Tourist Destinations

Places to visit in Lesotho

Waterfalls

Maletsunyane Falls

Semonkong: A 192 m waterfall. You can abseil beside it.

Waterfalls

Ketane Falls

Highlands: A 122 m waterfall reached by a hike or pony trek.

Waterfalls

Qiloane Falls

Thaba-Bosiu: A waterfall next to the hill that inspired the Basotho hat.

Culture

Thaba-Bosiu

Maseru: The mountain fortress of King Moshoeshoe I.

Culture

Morija Museum

Morija: Basotho history and nearby dinosaur footprints.

Culture

Matsieng Footprints

Maseru: Mythical footprints in the rock.

Culture

Ha Kome Caves

Berea: Cave houses built into the cliffs.

Culture

Liphofung Cave

Butha-Buthe: San rock paintings and a cave used by Moshoeshoe I.

Culture

Basotho Hat

Maseru: A hat-shaped craft centre for souvenirs.

Nature

Sehlabathebe National Park

Qachas Nek: A remote World Heritage park with rock art.

Nature

Tsehlanyane National Park

Leribe: Forest, streams, hiking and horse riding.

Nature

Bokong Nature Reserve

Leribe: Lepaqoa Falls and a visitor centre at 3,090 m.

Adventure

Sani Pass

Mokhotlong: A steep 4x4 road to the highest pub in Africa.

Adventure

Afriski Resort

Butha-Buthe: Skiing from June to August.

Adventure

Mafika Lisiu Pass

Highlands: One of the highest road passes in Africa.

Adventure

Thabana Ntlenyana

Mokhotlong: The highest mountain in southern Africa (3,482 m).

Dams

Katse Dam

Highlands: One of the highest dams in Africa (185 m).

Dams

Mohale Dam

Highlands: A big dam with a beautiful lake.
